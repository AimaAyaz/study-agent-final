Activation functions introduce non-linearity into neural networks. Without them, the network would behave like a simple linear model.

Common activation functions:
- ReLU: max(0, x). Fast and widely used.
- Sigmoid: squashes values between 0 and 1. Used in binary classification.
- Tanh: outputs between -1 and 1. Zero-centered.
- Softmax: converts logits into probabilities for multi-class classification.

Why needed:
They help the network learn complex patterns.
